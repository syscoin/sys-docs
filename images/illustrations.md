# Illustrating Syscoin

Use landscape ink and gouache on warm paper, deep indigo shadows and practical amber light. Give each scene a distinct viewpoint and one focal action that remains legible on a phone. A person reading a meter makes the distinction between a physical observation and a ledger entry visible.

Captions identify imagined settings. Keep original figures with their source and units. Draw protocol diagrams from the specification so each arrow corresponds to an operation a reader can check.

## Art and source prompts

The illustrations use the built-in image generation tool. The cooperative is the visual reference for the series. The later prompts combine this shared direction with an individual scene description.

> Create a distinct landscape plate for the same Syscoin technical documentation series as the reference. Match the reference's fine hand-inked contours, richly layered gouache on warm paper, amber light against deep indigo, and observational, documentary tone. Compose a wide horizontal landscape with one legible focal action. This is a different scene, not a restaging of the example. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no crypto icons, no diagram.

### Governing shared power

[governing-shared-power.webp](../static/img/governing-shared-power.webp)

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a wide 16:9 editorial illustration for the Syscoin documentation page about community governance. A small group of community members in a municipal workshop choose how to share a limited power supply. In the foreground, one technician points to a branching electrical plan on a worn table, another records the decision, and a third looks from the plan to the working equipment. In the background a modest battery cabinet and a row of conduit make the physical constraint clear. Understated editorial painted realism with tactile texture, ink details, warm practical light, and deep indigo shadows. Keep the focal action legible at mobile size. People are making a decision together. No logos, readable text, money, ballots, robots, holograms, or stock photography poses.

> Revise this same Mars illustration for physical accuracy. Both people are outdoors in the Martian atmosphere. Give each a sealed EVA pressure suit with a rigid helmet and reflective face visor, gloves, boots and a life-support pack. No exposed face, hair or skin on either person. Preserve the horizon, communications dish, warm habitat light, wide ink-and-gouache composition and all other details.

### Independent ChainLock verification

[pq-local-verification.webp](../static/img/pq-local-verification.webp)

> A matched pair of documentary views from two different places, separated by a narrow natural shadow. On the left an older woman in her modest city apartment at night watches her small desktop full node check an incoming certificate. On the right, on a different clock and in daylight, a younger man working alone at a countryside radio workshop checks the same certificate with his own small computer. Each of the two displays has the same simple three bands of tiny dots as an editorial visual rhyme, no letters or numbers. The people look at their own screens, never at each other. Two modest general-purpose computers. Distinct benches, windows and lighting. Put the screens and people's profiles close enough to see on a phone. Convey locally checkable evidence without depicting people hand-signing anything, specialized hardware, a giant central server or a third-party seal.

### Publishing and proving a zkSYS batch

[zksys-batch-paths.webp](../static/img/zksys-batch-paths.webp)

> An editorial panoramic cutaway of a community computing workshop doing two different jobs on the same batch. On the left, a data operator seats a removable drive at a densely shelved archive station. At center right, a proof worker leans over an open GPU server sled with several broad accelerator boards. Through the far window a third, modest monitoring workstation is visible with a short row of amber indicators on its screen, suggesting a settlement observer. Deliberately give the archive and prover equal visual weight. Eyes and hands checking the equipment are the action. Front three-quarter viewpoint, distinct work surfaces, indigo shadows, warm task lamps across the room. Make foreground storage and accelerator hardware recognizable even in a small thumbnail. The three stations are an imagined illustration of roles, without implying proofs move on paper, transfers by hand, or operator approval.
