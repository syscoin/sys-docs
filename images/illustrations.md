# Illustrating Syscoin

Use landscape ink and gouache on warm paper, deep indigo shadows and practical amber light. Give each scene a distinct viewpoint and one focal action that remains legible on a phone. A person reading a meter makes the distinction between a physical observation and a ledger entry visible.

Captions identify imagined settings. Keep original figures with their source and units. Draw protocol diagrams from the specification so each arrow corresponds to an operation a reader can check.

## Art and source prompts

The four illustrations use the built-in image generation tool. The cooperative is the visual reference for the other three. The later prompts combine this shared direction with an individual scene description.

> Create a distinct landscape plate for the same Syscoin technical documentation series as the reference. Match the reference's fine hand-inked contours, richly layered gouache on warm paper, amber light against deep indigo, and observational, documentary tone. Compose a wide horizontal landscape with one legible focal action. This is a different scene, not a restaging of the example. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no crypto icons, no diagram.

### Cooperative

[compute-cooperative.webp](../static/img/compute-cooperative.webp)

> Editorial illustration for Syscoin’s human and AI governance chapter. In a community-owned compute room at dusk, two cooperative members compare a paper reservation ledger with the physical power meter beside a single GPU rack. Landscape, precise ink and gouache on warm paper, deep indigo shadows, amber meter light, a quiet documentary composition. Keep the people, ledger, meter and rack legible on a phone. No lettering, logos, coins or robot mascots.

### Mining hall

[bitcoin-mining-hall.webp](../static/img/bitcoin-mining-hall.webp)

> At the entrance to an industrial Bitcoin mining hall in cool dawn light, a technician seen from behind wheels a spare fan toward long aisles of real ASIC miners. Warm amber light catches individual intake grilles and copper busbars. Wide architectural perspective, an ordinary person for scale, concrete floor and distant ventilation louvers. The physical hardware that earns proof of work is the subject.

### Recovery

[recovering-a-batch.webp](../static/img/recovering-a-batch.webp)

> Close still life from just above the shoulder of a local archive operator restoring a stored copy of a rollup batch. A hand slides a worn archival drive into a small desktop dock. In the background are a modest orderly shelf of removable drives and cool reflections off the dark monitor. Low angled late light falls across the drive in the foreground. Modest community workshop, tactile details and practical equipment.

### Across distance

[mars-signal.webp](../static/img/mars-signal.webp)

> Speculative observatory on Mars at first light. A geodesic habitat and an articulated communications dish at the edge of a human settlement in a broad red valley under a dark indigo sky. Two tiny figures adjust the dish toward a single planet-like point on the horizon. A distinct broad horizon, ochre dust, precise ink texture, warm quiet light from the habitat. A contemplation of distance and the wait for a signal, physically grounded without imaginary highways or beams.

The final pass corrected the outdoor work scene.

> Revise this same Mars illustration for physical accuracy. Both people are outdoors in the Martian atmosphere. Give each a sealed EVA pressure suit with a rigid helmet and reflective face visor, gloves, boots and a life-support pack. No exposed face, hair or skin on either person. Preserve the horizon, communications dish, warm habitat light, wide ink-and-gouache composition and all other details.
