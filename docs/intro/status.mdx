## Follow the evidence across services

- **A receipt identifies local execution.** Find its Edgechain batch, then look for the full publication on Core and the proof checked at settlement. Match the Edge execution event to its Gateway block and the enclosing Gateway batch to NEVM. A receiving contract has its own execution record. [Evidence at each stage](../architecture/overview.mdx#which-record-answers-which-question)
- **A commitment identifies data.** Verify a reachable copy against its digest. For a dispute after the serving window, an operator needs retained data, a known state and the replay inputs. [Evidence a week later](../architecture/data-availability.mdx#what-can-someone-verify-a-week-later)
- **Eligibility is the start of a payment check.** Identify the asset, accepted duty or block, and rules for its payee, then check what was actually issued, transferred, or burned. [Who pays whom](../architecture/modular-alignment.mdx#who-pays-for-the-shared-infrastructure)
- **An observer, signer, and rule administrator have different powers.** Verify the account’s grant and a revocation’s inclusion. To recover or leave a censored service, identify a deployed route and the authority that can enforce it. [Powers and remedies](./human-and-ai.mdx#if-the-operator-stops-cooperating)

For the argument connecting the layers, start with [Syscoin in a nutshell](./nutshell.mdx) or the [system map](../architecture/overview.mdx). To check a live service, use the [command reference](../reference/overview.mdx).
