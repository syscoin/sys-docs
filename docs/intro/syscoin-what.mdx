---
title: Choose where your application runs
sidebar_position: 3
description: Choose between a UTXO payment, a contract on NEVM, and execution on a zkSYS Edgechain.
---

| To build… | Use | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payments in SYS or Syscoin Platform Tokens | **Core** validates unspent transaction outputs (UTXOs) and their spending conditions. | [Core RPC](../reference/core.mdx) or [SyscoinJS](../dev-resources/documentation/javascript-sdk-ref/syscoinjs.mdx). For an eligible unconfirmed token transfer, see [Z-DAG](../dev-resources/sys/z-dag.mdx). |
| A Solidity application on Syscoin | **NEVM** executes contracts on mainnet and Tanenbaum. | [NEVM endpoints](../reference/nevm.mdx#endpoints-and-wallet-settings) and [EVM tooling](../dev-resources/nevm/tooling.mdx). |
| An application on the public zkSYS testnet | **Genesis** executes applications on an Edgechain. Batch data goes to Core, and settlement goes through Gateway to Tanenbaum NEVM. | [Genesis quickstart](../reference/zksys.mdx#start-on-zksys-genesis) and [batch lifecycle](../architecture/zksys.mdx#follow-an-application-transaction). |

NEVM and zkSYS Genesis use separate chain IDs and balances. Check the [network settings](./status.mdx) before connecting a wallet or choosing an RPC endpoint.
