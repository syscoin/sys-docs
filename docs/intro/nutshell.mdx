Syscoin is a Bitcoin-merge-mined blockchain. **Syscoin Core** processes SYS and UTXO asset transfers, accepts data publications, and enforces ChainLocks. **NEVM** runs Ethereum-compatible smart contracts. Core and NEVM run on mainnet. **zkSYS Genesis** is a public testnet for applications that execute on a separate chain and settle batches back to Syscoin. [Network status](./status.mdx) · [System architecture](../architecture/overview.mdx)

## Mission

An open source community dedicated to scaling Bitcoin, especially its proof-of-work security, to meet global needs and emerging use-cases, while sticking as close as possible to Bitcoin's original ideals.

## Current Research & Development

- **Post-quantum ChainLocks.** SLH-DSA authenticates Sentry operators, and WOTS+ keys sign scheduled ChainLock votes. [Certificate verification](../architecture/post-quantum.mdx#what-a-full-node-verifies)
- **zkSYS.** An Edgechain executes application transactions and publishes batch data to Core. Gateway settles Edgechain batches and submits its own batches to NEVM. [zkSYS architecture](../architecture/zksys.mdx) · [Genesis quickstart](../reference/zksys.mdx#start-on-zksys-genesis)
- **Post-quantum smart accounts.** An experimental SLH-DSA module authorizes operations for compatible zkSYS smart accounts. [Pali and zkSYS account signing](../architecture/zksys.mdx#wallets-and-post-quantum-account-authorization)
- **Bitcoin bridge.** The proposed bridge uses Syscoin block references recorded in Bitcoin coinbases to detect conflicting histories during a withdrawal. [Bitcoin bridge research](../architecture/bitcoin-bridge.mdx)

## Why build on Bitcoin mining?

A participating mining pool can reuse the same SHA-256 work to compete for rewards on Bitcoin and Syscoin. Core checks the parent work against Syscoin's target and validates Syscoin's transactions under its own rules. Sentry Nodes provide a separate finality mechanism by signing ChainLocks over Core blocks. [Merge mining and finality](../architecture/core.mdx)

## Follow the payment

A wallet sending a payment on zkSYS first gets a local execution receipt. The operator groups transactions into a batch, publishes its data on Core, and submits the batch for settlement. Gateway records the Edgechain batch, and NEVM settles the Gateway batch that contains that record.
