# Frequently asked questions

## Where do I connect?

Check [network status](/docs/intro/status), then use the matching [NEVM](/docs/reference/nevm), [zkSYS](/docs/reference/zksys), or [Core](/docs/reference/core) reference. Ethereum-compatible wallets identify networks by chain ID.

## What is the difference between PoDA and storing data forever?

A chain commitment identifies the expected payload. Core retains the payload according to its local protocol rules. Use [archive and retrieval procedures](/docs/architecture/data-availability) if your application needs older bytes.

## Which post-quantum features are deployed?

See the released and candidate boundaries in [Post-quantum ChainLocks](/docs/architecture/post-quantum) and [network status](/docs/intro/status).
