AI can find more ways to use the same water, power, and compute. Someone still has to decide who gets access, who can act on their behalf, and what happens when a decision is challenged. A shared ledger gives people a record they can check after an operator leaves or the people making the decision change. The community still has to agree on the rule and who can change it. ## Who can decide what?

| Where the decision is made | Who has authority | What others can check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Syscoin Core** · Mainnet public funding | Anyone can submit a proposal with the required fee. A valid Sentry Node’s registered voting key can vote on funding. Core enforces the rules for selecting and paying proposals. | Inspect the proposal and votes, then verify the actual payment in a superblock. [Proposals, votes, and commands](../guides/governance.mdx) |
| **zkSYS** · Reference contracts | Reward eligibility draws on SYS stake or eligible Sentry collateral. The zkSYS token supports delegated voting. The issuer, role administrators, and proxy administrator have specific powers under the installed contracts. | Inspect the contracts and their role holders. Each deployment determines who can issue, delegate, operate, and upgrade. [Funding, issuance, and authority](../architecture/modular-alignment.mdx#who-gets-paid-for-a-proved-batch) |
| **Applications** · Community design | A cooperative can set its own rules for membership, grants, revocation, and appeals. A wallet or application can enforce a grant limited to one resource, an amount, and an expiry. | Check who gave an agent authority, which rule the application applied, and what outcome it recorded. A meter reading needs an accountable source. [Grants, recovery, and appeals](../intro/human-and-ai.mdx#if-the-operator-stops-cooperating) |

## A budget that adjusts to what gets funded

Maintaining wallets, network services, and open-source software takes work even when markets are quiet. A fixed budget can be too small when several upgrades need funding at once. A single treasury also gives whoever controls it considerable influence over which work can continue. Syscoin relays public proposals and Sentry votes to participating nodes. It records the selected payouts on-chain, and the next budget responds to what was paid.

## Authority when agents act

An agent can search constraints, estimate costs, or draft a proposal. In the power cooperative, it could ask to reserve spare capacity for a member. To act on that request, it needs a grant the cooperative can revoke. If the meter was faulty or the rule omitted an emergency exception, people need a way to submit evidence and appeal. A proof can show which rule was applied to the recorded inputs. Resolving a disputed physical observation still takes an accountable process. [Truth states and remedies](../intro/human-and-ai.mdx#what-counts-as-a-truth-state) · [Jag Sidhu’s design essay](https://jsidhu.medium.com/truth-states-consciousness-and-decentralized-intelligence-d5415da47dc6)

These decisions can be local. Two cooperatives can each choose their own members, operators, and limits. They can agree on the rules for a shared resource and rely on common settlement for the outcomes that cross that boundary. That keeps application policy with the people living under it.
