Syscoin's funding process pays for work such as wallet maintenance, network services, and research. Anyone who pays the submission fee can propose work. Sentry Node votes determine which proposals qualify, and Syscoin Core enforces the payment rules.

- [Submit a proposal, cast a vote, or inspect a payment](../guides/governance.mdx)
- [See how the budget changes and verify a public payout](./dynamic-governance.mdx)

## Who can decide what?

| Scope | Authority | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Syscoin Core funding** | Each valid Sentry Node can vote on funding proposals through its registered voting key. Core limits which proposals a superblock can pay. | Proposal details, net support, and coinbase outputs. [Governance commands](../guides/governance.mdx) |
| **zkSYS reference contracts** | Each deployment grants specific powers to its issuers, role administrators, and proxy administrators. The zkSYS token supports delegated voting. | Deployed contracts, role holders, and upgrade permissions. [zkSYS rewards and authority](../architecture/modular-alignment.mdx#who-gets-paid-for-a-proved-batch) |
| **Application rules** | The application defines who can act and who can change its rules. It can delegate limited actions to accounts or automated agents. | The grant, policy version, and recorded outcome. [Delegation and recovery](../intro/human-and-ai.mdx#if-the-operator-stops-cooperating) |

## A budget that adjusts to what gets funded

Core adjusts the nominal funding budget within fixed bounds using the amount paid in a superblock. The next budget can rise, fall, or stay the same. A funding vote can pay for work on a protocol upgrade. Nodes enforce any new consensus rules once the upgrade activates. [Dynamic budget rules](./dynamic-governance.mdx)

## Authority when agents act

An application can limit an agent to a specific action, resource, amount, and expiry. Its governing rules determine who can revoke the grant, resolve disputes, and update the application. A proof can verify how the recorded inputs were processed. Resolving a disputed meter reading or equipment outage also takes evidence from people or devices.
